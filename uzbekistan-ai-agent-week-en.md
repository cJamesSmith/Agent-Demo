# AI Agent Training for the Uzbekistan Government Team

9–12 November, 14:00–17:00 local time each day. Participant packs are in `materials/`. The answer checks are in `materials/instructor-key.md` and stay with the instructor.

Groups of three stay together all week. Each afternoon uses only that day’s folder. If a fee, deadline, or legal rule is not in the pack, write “to be confirmed”. Centre No. 3, the meetings, and every figure are invented for the exercise. The process wording follows individual-entrepreneur registration on [my.gov.uz](https://my.gov.uz) and the public “start a business in 15 minutes” arrangement from January 2026: once an application is complete, registration takes no more than 30 minutes. An electronic signature, a bank account, and a cash register are outside that clock.

Tuesday to Thursday: 14:00–14:30 briefing, 14:30–16:00 group work, 16:00–17:00 presentations. A presentation states the conclusion, the source file, and one place a person changed the draft.

## Monday 9 November — a service page for Public Services Centre No. 3, Samarkand

14:00–14:20, agent concept from slides 3–6 of the previous deck. 14:20–15:05, WorkBuddy working folder and Ask / Plan / Craft; live-build a three-step page and fix one deliberate error. 15:05–16:20, build. 16:20–17:00, four minutes per group with the page open.

The task is in `materials/day1-website/`. Done means `index.html`. The page covers only three services: individual-entrepreneur registration, a PINFL, and an electronic-signature appointment. It states what to bring, the two channels (the desk and my.gov.uz), when the 30 minutes start, the address `12 Mirzo Ulugbek Street`, the phone `+998 66 555 01 03`, the hours, and an answer to each question in `resident-questions.txt`.

## Tuesday 10 November — the brief for tomorrow’s 10:00 meeting

The task is in `materials/day2-briefing/`. Participants are aides to the deputy of the Agency for Public Services. The question is why residents are not experiencing “15 minutes”. Every group has the same pack and a different brief: Economy ranks by days to operate, Digital by returns and the form, Social by access in Karakalpakstan. Give out one role per group at the start.

They answer where the time goes, which lines in `old-deck.md` the other files do not support, and what can be decided tomorrow versus what must wait. Done means a `briefing.md` of about two pages, with a source filename after each conclusion, and a `slides.md` of exactly six slides.

## Wednesday 11 November — one pilot this quarter

The task is in `materials/day3-budget/`. `regional-kpis.xlsx` holds May–October 2026 for four service areas: applications, days to operate, return rate, staff, spend, and satisfaction. The quarter can fund one action in one area: more staff, a simpler form, or a status-tracking page. The deputy currently puts speed first. The group also answers whether the choice holds if accuracy becomes first.

Done means a `dashboard.html` or `dashboard.xlsx` with a comparison table, one chart, and both priorities, plus a half-page `memo.md`. The last 20 minutes are for the second priority.

## Thursday 12 November — a home sewing workshop: public page, internal procedure, three letters

The task is in `materials/day4-service/`. Same centre as Monday. Three products:

- `public-page.html` — who may apply, what to bring, how the fee is stated, the steps, how to ask for status, how to appeal a refusal
- `internal-sop.md` — who does what on which day, when a file is overdue, when it goes to the supervisor
- `letters.md` — one acceptance, one request for documents, and one refusal, using `three-cases.md`

The public page and the internal procedure use the same number of days. A refusal names the reason from the pack. “Incomplete documents”, with no document named, is not a finished letter.
