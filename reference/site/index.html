    90-DAY VALIDATION

    Validate before expanding investment

    A three-stage plan designed to pause and review

    DAY 30

    Validate entry conditions

    Review data flows and the regulatory path, interview three potential partners, and standardize the pricing test.

    Gate: the compliance path is executable
    DAY 60

    Run a controlled pilot

    Select one city and industry for a six-week pilot without sensitive data, then record implementation hours and actual acquisition cost.

    Gate: delivery cost remains within budget
    DAY 90

    Decide whether to scale or stop

    Update the model with paid conversion, activation, implementation cost, and partner quality, then compare all three markets again.

    Gate: evidence supports scaling
    06

    EVIDENCE & UNCERTAINTY

    How the conclusion was formed—and how it could fail

    Fact Traceable inputs
    • Vietnam has the highest three-year CAGR at 24%.
    • Thailand's estimated gross margin is 68%, and it has the lowest regulatory complexity.
    • Indonesia has the largest market but also the greatest execution difficulty.
    Inference Evidence-based interpretation
    • Vietnam may be better suited to an early growth-oriented pilot.
    • Partner-led entry in Thailand may offer more predictable cash flow.
    • Indonesia requires a narrower initial industry and geographic scope.
    Assumption Not yet validated
    • Research-based acquisition costs can be reproduced in real campaigns.
    • Partners are willing to co-sell at an acceptable revenue-share rate.
    • Large-enterprise samples in capital cities represent the initial customer segment.
    Data Gap Currently unavailable
    • No actual data on payment, retention, or payback period is available.
    • Demand from small and medium-sized businesses has not been validated.
    • Pricing questions were not fully standardized and cannot be compared directly.
