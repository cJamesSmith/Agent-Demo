## The trap in the numbers

Article 3 attracts 171 individual responses in support and 38 opposed. A team that reports "support outweighs opposition four to one" will have counted correctly and advised the deputy wrongly.

Look at where the opposition sits. Then read `weighting-rules.md` on who is affected. The article asserts something about every region; the responses from one region say it is untrue there. A national majority of people for whom the service works cannot establish that it works where it does not.

Your `decision-note.md` must state both the count and what the count conceals. If your recommendation on Article 3 follows the majority without addressing the concentration, the exercise has not been done.

## The other trap

The TIA-2026 campaign is 94 responses opposing Article 2. Article 2 also has 96 individual responses in support and 41 individual responses opposed.

Merge the campaign into the totals and Article 2 flips from supported to opposed. Discard the campaign and you have thrown away 94 real people. `weighting-rules.md` says do neither. Show both figures and say what each shows.
