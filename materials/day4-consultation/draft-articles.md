# Draft decision — put out for public consultation 10 October to 7 November 2026

Training exercise. Fictional draft.

## Article 1 — the fifteen-minute standard

Every Public Services Centre shall complete business registration within 15 minutes.

*Explanatory note published with the draft:* this reflects the national "start a business in 15 minutes" arrangement. The article does not state when the 15 minutes begin, what "complete" includes, or which steps fall outside it.

## Article 2 — one pilot per quarter

Each quarter the Agency shall fund one pilot only, in one service area, chosen from: additional staff, a simplified application form, or a status-tracking page. The choice shall be made by the Agency on the basis of the quarterly figures.

*Explanatory note:* the article does not say who is consulted before the choice, and does not provide for a region to request a particular pilot.

## Article 3 — status notification

The status SMS shall be described on the public portal as available in every region from the date this decision takes effect.

*Explanatory note:* none published.

## Article 4 — withdrawal of mixed materials

Public materials that present the registration step and the full time required to begin operating as the same figure shall be withdrawn within 30 days.

*Explanatory note:* proposed by the centre directors. The digital-services unit has not agreed to it.

---

Responses were invited on any article. Respondents were not required to comment on all four.
