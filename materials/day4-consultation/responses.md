# Written comments — selected

Training exercise. Fictional respondents.

412 responses were received. 287 left written comments. The 35 below were selected by the consultation team as the substantive arguments; the rest repeat these points. The counts in `responses.csv` cover all 412 responses, including those with no written comment.

Each entry gives the response ID, region, respondent type, and the article the comment is about.

---

## On Article 1 — the fifteen-minute standard

**R-014 · Fergana Region · entrepreneur · oppose**
"Fifteen minutes from when? I spent three weeks getting to the point where the fifteen minutes could start. Write down when the clock starts or the article means nothing."

**R-031 · Tashkent City · resident · oppose**
"The registration click was quick. It is everything around it that took a fortnight. An article that regulates only the quick part will not change what I experienced."

**R-058 · Samarkand Region · centre_staff · oppose**
"We cannot comply with this. Not because we are slow — a complete file is often done the same morning — but because the article does not define 'complete', so any file we return for correction can be called a breach. You are creating a standard we can fail without doing anything wrong."

**R-077 · Republic of Karakalpakstan · regional_government · oppose**
"Nine officers cover this region. The article applies the same figure to us as to Tashkent City with fifty. We will not meet it and the figure will be used to say we underperform."

**R-102 · Tashkent City · business_association · support**
"The standard is right. Keep the figure. Add one sentence defining when it starts and the objection disappears."

**R-133 · Fergana Region · resident · oppose**
"The problem is not the time. It is being sent back three times without being told everything wrong on the first visit."

**R-189 · Samarkand Region · centre_staff · oppose**
"Define 'complete' in the article itself, not in a note. Notes are not binding and we are audited against the article."

**R-204 · Tashkent City · resident · support**
"Mine took eleven minutes. Keep it."

**R-247 · Republic of Karakalpakstan · ngo · oppose**
"A resident four hours from Nukus does not care about fifteen minutes. She cares whether the journey was necessary."

## On Article 2 — one pilot per quarter

**R-022 · Fergana Region · entrepreneur · oppose (campaign TIA-2026)**
"One pilot per quarter across the whole country is not a reform programme. Our association asked members to say so."

**R-069 · Republic of Karakalpakstan · regional_government · oppose**
"The article gives a region no way to ask for what it needs. We know we need staff. We will find out in a circular whether we are getting a status page instead."

**R-094 · Samarkand Region · centre_staff · support**
"One pilot done properly beats four done badly. We have lived through four at once. Keep the limit."

**R-118 · Tashkent City · ngo · oppose**
"'On the basis of the quarterly figures' decides nothing. Which figures, and who breaks a tie? Write the criterion into the article."

**R-156 · Fergana Region · business_association · support**
"Support, with one change: publish why the chosen pilot was chosen. The constraint is fine. The silence is not."

**R-201 · Samarkand Region · resident · support**
"I would rather one thing worked than three were announced."

**R-288 · Republic of Karakalpakstan · ngo · oppose**
"Three quarters of pilots will go to the regions with the best data. We have the worst data because we have the fewest staff to record it."

## On Article 3 — status notification

**R-008 · Republic of Karakalpakstan · resident · oppose**
"This is false. I have not received an SMS. My neighbour has not received an SMS. You are proposing to publish on the portal that a thing exists here which does not exist here."

**R-045 · Republic of Karakalpakstan · regional_government · oppose**
"We have reported since September that SMS is not delivered in this region. If this article takes effect, our desks will be telling residents to expect a message we know will not arrive."

**R-083 · Tashkent City · resident · support**
"Mine works. I got three messages."

**R-127 · Republic of Karakalpakstan · ngo · oppose**
"Thirty of our thirty-four members in this region report no SMS. Publishing otherwise is not an administrative error, it is telling people something untrue in writing."

**R-164 · Samarkand Region · resident · support**
"Works here. Useful."

**R-198 · Republic of Karakalpakstan · resident · oppose**
"I came to the centre three times because no message came. The article would make my experience officially impossible."

**R-235 · Fergana Region · centre_staff · support**
"It works in our region. But I would not write 'every region' unless somebody has checked every region. Has anyone asked Nukus?"

**R-301 · Republic of Karakalpakstan · entrepreneur · oppose**
"Change 'every region' to the regions where it has been verified. That is all that is needed."

## On Article 4 — withdrawal of mixed materials

**R-111 · Tashkent City · resident · support**

**R-223 · Samarkand Region · resident · support**

**R-259 · Republic of Karakalpakstan · centre_staff · support**

"Saturday closing. I work six days. The draft does not mention opening hours."

**R-333 · Fergana Region · centre_staff**
