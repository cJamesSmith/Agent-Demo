# Budget note — one pilot this quarter

Training exercise. Invented policy constraint.

The envelope pays for one of the three options below, in one service area. Do not invent a som price.

## More staff

Four additional officers for three months, sent to one service area.

Main effect: lower `avg_days_to_operate` where each officer handles a heavy load of applications. Little effect on the return-for-correction rate.

## Simpler form

One revised application and a one-day refresher for intake staff in one service area.

Main effect: lower return-for-correction rate. Little effect where that rate is already under 15 percent.

## Status-tracking page

A status page and an SMS when the file moves, in one service area.

Main effect: higher satisfaction and fewer visits made only to ask “where is my file”. It does not fix a missing address or a wrong activity code.
