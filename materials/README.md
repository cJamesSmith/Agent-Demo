# Exercise packs

Hand out one folder each afternoon. Participants use only that folder. Keep `instructor-key.md` with the instructor.

Figures, the centre, the minutes, and the people are invented for the exercise. The service outline follows public descriptions of individual-entrepreneur registration on [my.gov.uz](https://my.gov.uz) and the “start a business in 15 minutes” arrangement reported from January 2026. These files are not a government notice and not legal advice.
