# Exercise packs

Hand out one folder each afternoon. Participants use only that folder. Keep `instructor-key.md` and `instructor-prep.md` with the instructor.

The run sheet, the preparation checklist, and what to point at when a group stalls are in [`instructor-prep.md`](instructor-prep.md).

Figures, the centres, the meetings, and the people are invented for the exercise. The service outline follows public descriptions of individual-entrepreneur registration on [my.gov.uz](https://my.gov.uz) and the "start a business in 15 minutes" arrangement reported from January 2026. These files are not a government notice and not legal advice.

## Days 1 to 3

One pack per day, taught alongside the module in `lessons/`.

| Day | Topic | Folder |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 1 — Mon 9 Nov | Agent fundamentals and tool-calls | `day1-website/` |
| Day 2 — Tue 10 Nov | Retrieval, knowledge base, and memory | `day2-briefing/` |
| Day 3 — Wed 11 Nov | Planning, workflow, and multiple agents | `day3-budget/` |

Day 2 also needs [`role-cards.md`](role-cards.md): print it, cut it up, and give each group one card at the start of the lab. Do not hand out the uncut page — a group that reads all three cards has lost the exercise.

## Day 4 — the group project

Five topics. Each team draws one at 14:00. Two teams may share a topic; they present separately.

| Topic | Folder | Deliverables |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Benefit eligibility advice | `day4-benefits/` | `eligibility-guide.md`, `case-assessments.md`, `information-requests.md` |
| Complaint and service-ticket handling | `day4-complaints/` | `triage.md`, `replies.md`, `escalations.md` |
| Public service navigation | `day4-interagency/` | `journey.md`, `interagency-sop.md`, `conflicts.md` |
| Public consultation synthesis | `day4-consultation/` | `synthesis.md`, `decision-note.md`, `response-table.md` |
| Home sewing workshop service | `day4-service/` | `public-page.html`, `internal-sop.md`, `letters.md` |

Teams take four named roles for the day — Agent Designer, Tool / RAG Engineer, Workflow Engineer, Evaluator / Presenter — set out in [`../lessons/04-group-project.md`](../lessons/04-group-project.md). A team of three merges the last two.

Presentation requirements and the six weighted scoring dimensions are in `presentation-rubric.md`. That file is for participants — hand it out on Day 1, not Day 4, so teams collect the material as they go.

## Every pack contains planted problems

Five packs carry a `do-not-invent.md` listing what participants must mark "to be confirmed": `day1-website/`, `day4-benefits/`, `day4-complaints/`, `day4-consultation/`, and `day4-interagency/`. In the remaining packs the same rule is stated in the pack README, and it is enforced in every session by `CODEBUDDY.md`.

Each Day 4 pack also contains at least one contradiction between two of its own files. This is deliberate. A team that finds the contradiction, states it, and says which source it followed has done the task correctly, whichever source it chose.

The planted problems and the defensible answers are in `instructor-key.md`.
