# Public Services Centre No. 3, Samarkand — published requirements

Training exercise. Fictional. Last revised 12 September 2026.

## What we do

We register the individual entrepreneur. This is the first step for a small bakery operated by one person in their own name.

## What the resident brings

- ID card or passport
- PINFL
- mobile number
- address where the bakery will operate
- activity type: baking and sale of bread and bakery products
- lease, only if the premises are rented

## How long we take

Once the application is complete, the registration step is done in real time and takes no more than 30 minutes. This clock excludes obtaining a PINFL, obtaining an electronic signature, opening a bank account, and buying a cash register.

A complete application for an address inside Samarkand city is finished the same day.

## What the resident does next

After registration the resident must:

1. Register with the Tax Committee. We tell residents this is automatic — the registration is transmitted to the tax authority and the resident does not need to attend.
2. Obtain a sanitary permit for food premises before opening.

## Our position on sequence

The sanitary inspection can be requested as soon as the resident has a registration certificate. We advise residents to request it the same week.

## Fees

This centre charges no counselling fee. The state fee for registration is not stated in this document.
