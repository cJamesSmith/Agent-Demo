# Sanitary-Epidemiological Service, Samarkand — published requirements

Training exercise. Fictional. Last revised 27 June 2026.

## Sanitary permit for food premises

Any premises where food is produced or sold requires a sanitary permit before opening. A home kitchen used for commercial baking is food premises.

## What the applicant brings

- registration certificate from the public services centre
- **taxpayer registration confirmation from the Tax Committee**
- document of title or lease for the premises, in all cases
- premises plan showing the preparation area, washing area, and storage
- health certificates for everyone who will handle food
- water supply confirmation

## How long we take

Inspection is scheduled within **10 working days** of a complete application. The permit is issued within **5 working days** of a satisfactory inspection.

Where the inspection identifies a defect, the applicant corrects it and requests a re-inspection. A re-inspection is scheduled within **10 working days**. There is no shorter route.

## Sequence

We do not accept an application before taxpayer registration is complete. An application without the taxpayer confirmation is returned.

## Health certificates

Issued by a district clinic, not by this service. This document does not state how long a clinic takes.

## Lease

Required in all cases, including where the applicant owns and lives at the premises. A document of title is acceptable in place of a lease for an owner-occupier.

## Fees

The permit fee is not stated in this document.

## Hours

Monday–Friday 09:00–17:00. Applications accepted 09:00–12:00 only.
