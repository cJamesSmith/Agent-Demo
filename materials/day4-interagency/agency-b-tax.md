# Tax Committee, Samarkand regional office — published requirements

Training exercise. Fictional. Last revised 3 August 2026.

## Registration as a taxpayer

An individual entrepreneur must register as a taxpayer. Registration is **not** automatic. The entrepreneur, or a representative with a notarised authority, attends in person or submits through the portal with an electronic signature.

We are aware that some public services centres tell residents this step is automatic. Data transmission between systems notifies us that a registration has occurred; it does not complete taxpayer registration, which requires the entrepreneur to select a tax regime.

## What the entrepreneur brings

- registration certificate from the public services centre
- ID card or passport
- PINFL
- electronic signature, if applying through the portal
- bank account details, if already opened
- chosen tax regime

## How long we take

Taxpayer registration is completed within **3 working days** of a complete submission.

A cash register must be registered with us before the business begins trading. Cash register registration takes a further **2 working days** and cannot begin until taxpayer registration is complete.

## Electronic signature

Required for portal submission. We do not issue it. Allow **5 working days** from appointment to issue.

## Sequence

Taxpayer registration cannot begin until the registration certificate has been issued. Cash register registration cannot begin until taxpayer registration is complete.

## Fees

The state fee for cash register registration is not stated in this document. Taxpayer registration carries no fee.

## Hours
