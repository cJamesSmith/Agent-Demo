# Three residents who went through it

Training exercise. Fictional residents. Collected by the working group in October 2026.

These are what happened, not what the published rules say should happen. Where a diary and an agency document disagree, both are evidence and both belong in `conflicts.md`.

---

## Shahlo Umarova — home bakery, Samarkand city — 41 days

**Day 1.** Centre. Complete file, registered before lunch. Told the tax registration was automatic and she did not need to do anything.

**Day 9.** Went to request the sanitary permit. Turned away: no taxpayer registration confirmation. This was the first she had heard that taxpayer registration was something she had to do.

**Day 10.** Tax Committee. Could not submit through the portal — no electronic signature. Made an appointment for the signature.

**Day 17.** Electronic signature issued.

**Day 18.** Tax Committee, portal submission.

**Day 21.** Taxpayer registration complete.

**Day 22.** Sanitary application refused at the counter: no health certificate. Nobody had mentioned health certificates. Arrived at 12:40 and was told applications close at 12:00.

**Day 23.** District clinic. Health certificate issued in 6 days.

**Day 29.** Sanitary application accepted, 09:30.

**Day 38.** Inspection. Passed.

**Day 41.** Permit issued. Opened day 42.

> "Every single body did its own part on time. I lost a month between them."

## Ilhom Qodirov — home bakery, Samarkand city — 63 days

Same route as Shahlo as far as taxpayer registration, which completed on day 19.

**Day 24.** Sanitary application accepted.

**Day 33.** Inspection. Failed: no separate washing area.

**Day 34–41.** Built the washing area.

**Day 42.** Requested re-inspection.

**Day 55.** Re-inspection. Passed.

**Day 60.** Permit issued.

**Day 61.** Discovered he could not trade without a registered cash register, and that it requires completed taxpayer registration first. Registered it in 2 days.

**Day 63.** Opened.

> "Nobody told me about the cash register until the permit was already in my hand. It could have been done in week three while I was waiting for the inspection."

## Mavluda Ergasheva — home bakery, Samarkand city — 27 days

An aunt who had been through it told her the order in advance.

**Day 1.** Centre. Registered.

**Day 1, afternoon.** Booked the electronic-signature appointment and went to the district clinic the same day for health certificates.

**Day 6.** Health certificates issued.

**Day 8.** Electronic signature issued.

**Day 9.** Tax Committee submission.

**Day 12.** Taxpayer registration complete. Requested cash register registration the same day.

**Day 13.** Sanitary application accepted, arrived 09:15 with everything including the title document.

**Day 14.** Cash register registered.

**Day 24.** Inspection. Passed.

**Day 27.** Permit issued. Opened day 28.

> "I did not do anything faster than the others. I just did things at the same time instead of one after another. Everything I know I learned from my aunt, not from any of the three offices."

## Working group note

Of 34 residents surveyed, 29 were told at the centre that tax registration was automatic. 31 did not know about health certificates until they were refused at the sanitary counter. No resident reported being given the order of steps in writing by any of the three bodies.
