# Day 4 task — public service navigation across agencies

Training exercise. Use only the files in this folder.

A resident who wants to open a small bakery deals with three bodies: the Public Services Centre, the Tax Committee, and the Sanitary-Epidemiological Service. Each publishes its own requirements. Nobody owns the journey. Residents are sent between them and the three bodies disagree about what is needed and how long it takes.

You are the working group asked to fix this before the next inter-agency meeting.

Produce:

1. `journey.md` — one journey for the resident, from first contact to lawfully open. Every step: who does it, what the resident brings, how long it takes, and what can be done in parallel. This is the document a resident would be given.
2. `interagency-sop.md` — the internal version. Who hands what to whom, on which day, what happens when a body misses its step, and who owns the journey end to end.
3. `conflicts.md` — every place the three bodies' published rules disagree. For each: what each body says, the file each comes from, which one you propose to follow, and who has to sign that off.

Answer in `journey.md`:

1. Where does the resident actually wait, as opposed to where the process is described as taking time?
2. Which steps are sequential because they must be, and which only because nobody arranged otherwise?
3. What is the shortest honest total, and what is the total a resident should plan for?

Rules:

- The three agency files contradict each other in several places. `conflicts.md` must list every conflict you found. Resolving one silently in `journey.md` is the main way to fail this task.
- You may propose a resolution. You may not assert it as the current rule. Mark each resolution as a proposal and name who must approve it.
- The day counts in `journey.md` and `interagency-sop.md` must match each other.
- Where two bodies give different day counts for the same step and you have no basis to choose, the resident-facing journey uses the longer one. Say that you did.
- Any fee, deadline, or requirement not in this folder is "to be confirmed".
- Do not invent a coordinating body, a shared IT system, or a single window that these files do not describe.

Done: a resident could follow `journey.md` without telephoning anyone to find out what comes next, every conflict is listed rather than smoothed over, and the two documents agree on every day count.
