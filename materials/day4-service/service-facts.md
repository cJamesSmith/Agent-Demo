# Service facts — home sewing workshop

Training exercise. Fictional centre rules for this exercise. Not a legal notice.

Service name: register a home sewing workshop as an individual entrepreneur.

Who may apply: a resident who will sew clothes in his or her own name at one address in Samarkand city. Registering a company is a different service and is not offered on this page.

Bring:

- ID card or passport
- PINFL
- mobile number
- address of the workshop
- activity type: sewing / making clothes

Lease: required only if the room is rented. Not required if the applicant lives at that address.

Channels: Desk A at Public Services Centre No. 3, 12 Mirzo Ulugbek Street, Samarkand, +998 66 555 01 03; or online after OneID, via business registration on my.gov.uz.

Hours: Monday–Friday 09:00–17:00; Saturday 09:00–13:00; Sunday closed. Desk A stays open through lunch.

Clock already agreed:

- A complete application for an address inside Samarkand city is finished on the same day.
- The “no more than 30 minutes” line applies only after the application is complete. It does not include PINFL, electronic signature, bank account, or cash register.
- If the address is outside Samarkand city, Desk A does not register it. The supervisor transfers the file within 2 working days.

Fee:

- This centre charges no counselling fee.
- The state fee in som is not in this pack.

Status and appeal:

- The pack does not say that SMS is available.
- A resident who wants to know the status comes to Desk B or calls the centre phone.
- A refusal can be appealed to the centre supervisor. This pack does not state the appeal deadline in days.
