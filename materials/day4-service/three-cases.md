# Three cases for the three letters

Training exercise. Fictional people.

## 1. Dilnoza Karimova — write an acceptance

- Lives at 7 Bogishamol Street, Samarkand city, and will sew clothes there.
- Has an ID card, PINFL, and a mobile number.
- The home is hers. There is no lease, and none is required.
- Activity: sewing / making clothes.
- She applied at Desk A this morning. The file is complete.

## 2. Jasur Tursunov — ask for more documents

- Wants to sew clothes in a rented room at 4 Uzbekiston Street, Samarkand city.
- Has an ID card and PINFL.
- Told Desk A the room is rented. He did not bring a lease.
- Applied today. The hold for a missing document is 5 working days.

## 3. Nilufar Yusupova — refuse with a reason

- Wants to register at this service to sell medicines from a kiosk.
- Has an ID card, PINFL, and a shop address in Samarkand city.
- Selling medicines is not on the allowed list for this service.
