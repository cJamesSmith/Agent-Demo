# Desk rota — Centre No. 3

Training exercise.

| Who | When | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Desk A, intake | Mon–Sat, centre hours | Checks identity, PINFL, activity, address, and lease if the room is rented. Accepts a complete city file the same day. |
| Desk B, status | Mon–Fri 09:00–17:00 | Answers “where is my file”. Does not decide a refusal. |
| Supervisor | Mon–Fri 14:00–17:00 | Takes files that Desk A cannot finish: address outside the city, activity not on the allowed list, or a file still open at the end of day 2. |

Overdue: a Samarkand-city file that is still open at the close of the next working day goes to the supervisor. A file with one missing document is held for 5 working days, then closed if the document has not arrived.
