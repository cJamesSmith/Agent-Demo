# Low-income family allowance — conditions and documents

Internal reference, family allowance desk, Public Service Centre No. 3, Samarkand. Issued 1 September 2026. Supersedes the version of 4 March 2026.

Invented for a training exercise.

## Who may apply

An adult resident applying on behalf of a household in which they live. One application per household.

The applicant must apply in person at the desk or through the portal using their own electronic signature. A relative may not apply on someone's behalf, and the desk may not accept an application from a neighbour, an employer, or a mahalla representative.

## The conditions

The allowance is assessed on **household income per person** against the threshold below. No other financial test applies.

| Condition | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Household income per person | At or below 620,000 som per month, averaged over the three calendar months before the application |
| Household composition | At least one child under 18, or one household member with a confirmed disability |
| Applicant's residence | Registered residence in the Republic of Uzbekistan |
| Existing allowance | The household is not already receiving this allowance |

Household income per person is the total monthly household income divided by the number of people registered as living at the address. Income includes wages, pension, existing benefit payments, and declared income from individual entrepreneurial activity.

The three-month average is used to avoid refusing a household on the strength of one unusual month. Where a household has income in only some of the three months, the average is still taken over three.

## Documents required

1. The applicant's identity document.
2. The applicant's PINFL.
3. A household composition certificate, issued by the mahalla committee, showing every person registered at the address.
4. Income confirmation for each earning member of the household, covering the three calendar months before the application. An employer's certificate, a pension statement, or a tax declaration for entrepreneurial income are all accepted.
5. For a household qualifying on disability, the confirming medical document.

A household with no earning member submits a written statement to that effect in place of item 4. The statement is made on the desk's form and signed in person.

## How the application is decided

The desk checks the documents, records the application, and forwards it to the district commission. The commission decides. The desk does not decide, and an officer must not tell a resident their application will be approved.

## Refusals

A refusal must state the condition that was not met. A household refused on income may apply again when its three-month average changes.

The appeal route and the deadline for appealing are to be confirmed. This has been raised with the regional office and no instruction has been received.

## What this document does not cover

- The monthly payment amount. Payment amounts are set centrally and are not issued to the desk in writing.
- Any allowance other than the low-income family allowance.
- Households where no member holds a registered residence.
