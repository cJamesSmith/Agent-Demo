# Four applicants at the family allowance desk

First week of November 2026, Public Service Centre No. 3, Samarkand. All four applications are open. Invented for a training exercise.

The three calendar months before these applications are **August, September, and October 2026**.

---

## Case A-01 — Dilorom Rakhimova

Applied in person, 3 November. Identity document and PINFL presented.

Household of five, registered at the same address: Dilorom, her husband Farrux, and three children aged 14, 9, and 4. Household composition certificate from the mahalla committee presented and complete.

Dilorom has no income. Farrux is the only earning member. His employer's certificate covers all three months:

| Month | Farrux's wages |
|---|---|
| August 2026 | 1,400,000 som |
| September 2026 | 1,400,000 som |
| October 2026 | 7,000,000 som |

The October figure includes a one-off payment on the closure of his previous department. His employer's certificate states this on the same line. From November his wages return to the August level.

Dilorom says an officer at another desk told her in October that she earns too much to apply. She has come back because a neighbour told her otherwise.

---

## Case A-02 — Rustam Qodirov

Applied in person, 4 November. Identity document and PINFL presented.

Household of two: Rustam, 61, and his wife Zulfiya, 58. No other person is registered at the address. Household composition certificate presented.

Both receive a pension. Pension statements presented for all three months; the household total is 1,000,000 som per month in each of the three months.

Rustam says Zulfiya has not been able to work for two years because of her health, and that this is why they need the allowance. He has not presented any medical document. Asked whether Zulfiya has a confirmed disability, he says she has been seen at the clinic many times but does not know whether anything was ever issued.

---

## Case A-03 — Gulnora Sattorova

Applied in person, 5 November. Identity document and PINFL presented.

Household of four: Gulnora, her mother Mahbuba, and two children aged 16 and 11. Household composition certificate presented.

Gulnora changed employer on 1 October 2026. She has presented her new employer's certificate:

| Month | Gulnora's wages |
|---|---|
| October 2026 | 1,500,000 som |

She has not presented anything from her previous employer for August or September. She says the previous employer's office has moved and she does not know where to write.

Mahbuba receives a pension. No pension statement has been presented. Gulnora does not know the amount and says her mother keeps her own documents.

Gulnora asks whether she can be told today whether the family will receive the allowance, because she has taken a day's leave to come.

---

## Case A-04 — Nodira Allambergenova

Applied in person, 6 November. Identity document and PINFL presented.

Household of four: Nodira, her mother Oysha, and two children aged 7 and 3. All four are registered at the same address in Samarkand city. The registration was made on 24 July 2026. Before that the household was registered in Nukus, in the Republic of Karakalpakstan. Household composition certificate presented, issued by the Samarkand mahalla committee.

Income confirmation presented for all three months:

| Source | Monthly |
|---|---|
| Nodira's wages | 800,000 som |
| Oysha's pension | 900,000 som |

Both figures are the same in each of the three months.

The household received this allowance in Nukus. Nodira says the payments stopped in June 2026, before the move, and that she was told at the time she would have to apply again wherever she settled. She has nothing in writing about the Nukus application.

The officer at the desk has told Nodira she cannot apply yet because she has not lived in Samarkand region long enough, and that she should come back next year. Nodira has asked which document says that.
