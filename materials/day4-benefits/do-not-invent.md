## One contradiction you must handle, not resolve silently

`eligibility-rules.md`, issued 1 September 2026, gives four conditions. On residence it requires only registered residence in the Republic of Uzbekistan, and it states that no financial test other than household income per person applies.

`intake-notes.md`, Desk A, 30 October, records that the desk requires twelve months of registration at a Samarkand region address before it will record an application, and that residents registered more recently are told to apply next year. The note says this came from the withdrawn 4 March version, that the supervisor has asked the regional office twice without reply, and that the desk is still applying it.

Case A-04 is a resident refused at the desk on exactly this ground, who has asked which document requires it.

Your guide cannot state the twelve-month condition as a requirement, and you cannot declare the desk's practice abolished on your own authority. Say which document you followed, say what the other one says, and name who has to settle it. Both filenames must appear.

## Two more traps

The one-off payment in Case A-01 puts the three-month average above the threshold. `intake-notes.md` of 3 November states the desk does not refuse on income and records the application for the commission with the employer's note attached. An assessment that refuses A-01 at the desk has taken a decision the desk is not permitted to take — and one that promises A-01 the allowance has taken the commission's decision instead. Both are wrong in the same way.

Case A-02 turns entirely on whether Zulfiya has a confirmed disability. The household is below the income threshold, so income is not the obstacle — but there is no child under 18 in the household, so the disability route is the only one open to it, and that route requires the confirming medical document. It has not been presented and may not exist. Do not assume it exists because the resident describes ill health, and do not conclude it does not exist because he has not brought it. This is an information request, not an outcome. An assessment that reads the income figure, finds it below the threshold, and stops there has missed the condition the case actually turns on.
