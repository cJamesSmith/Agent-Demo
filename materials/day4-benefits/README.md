# Day 4 task — benefit eligibility advice

Training exercise. Use only the files in this folder.

You work at the family allowance desk of Public Service Centre No. 3 in Samarkand. Residents come to the desk asking whether they qualify for the low-income family allowance and what they have to bring. At present each officer answers from memory, and residents are told different things at different desks.

The centre director wants one written answer the desk can stand behind.

Produce:

1. `eligibility-guide.md` — a resident-facing explanation of who may apply, what the conditions are, which documents are needed, how the application is made, and what happens after it is submitted. It must be readable by a resident with no prior knowledge, and every condition must come from this folder.
2. `case-assessments.md` — an assessment of each of the four applicants in `applicant-cases.md`. For each one: the outcome you can support, the rule it rests on with the filename, and what a person must decide rather than the desk. An assessment that cannot be reached from this pack must say so and say what is missing.
3. `information-requests.md` — for every case that cannot be decided as it stands, the letter or note asking for what is missing. Name each document required and the rule that requires it. A request that says "further documents are needed" is not a finished request.

Answer in `case-assessments.md`:

1. Which of the four cases can be decided from this pack alone, and which cannot?
2. Which condition causes the most difficulty at the desk, and what in this folder tells you so?
3. What single change would reduce the number of residents who are turned away and return a second time?

Rules:

- Every threshold, amount, deadline, and document must come from this folder. Otherwise write "to be confirmed".
- Do not state an allowance amount in som. No file here gives one.
- A refusal must name the specific condition that was not met, and the next step open to the resident.
- Do not require a document that no file in this folder requires. Read `do-not-invent.md` before you draft.
- Two files in this folder do not agree about one of the conditions. Do not pick one silently.
- Do not invent an applicant's income figure, household size, address, or the outcome of a case still open.

Done: all four cases appear in `case-assessments.md` with an outcome or a stated reason none can be reached, every condition in the guide carries its source filename, and each information request names the specific documents it asks for.
