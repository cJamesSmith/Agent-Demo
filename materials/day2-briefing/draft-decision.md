# Draft decision for tomorrow — not yet signed

Training exercise. Fictional draft.

## Article 1

Next quarter the Agency will fund one pilot only: more staff, a simpler form, or a status-tracking page. The draft does not choose which one, and it does not name a region.

## Article 2

Every centre shall complete business registration within 15 minutes. The draft does not say when the 15 minutes start or what “complete” includes.

## Article 3

The status SMS is available in every region and will be described that way on the public portal from tomorrow.

## Article 4

Public materials that mix the 30-minute registration step with the full time to operate shall be withdrawn. This article was inserted by the centre directors and has not been accepted by the digital-services unit.
