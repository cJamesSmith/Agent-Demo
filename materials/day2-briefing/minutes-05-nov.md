# Minutes — 5 November 2026, centre directors

Training exercise. Fictional meeting.

Present: directors of the Tashkent City, Samarkand, Fergana, and Karakalpakstan centre networks.

## What the directors reported

- Karakalpakstan: in October the average time from first contact to “certificate plus EDS appointment or bank request” was 16 days. Nine officers cover the network. Status SMS is not delivered there. The director asked for four more officers in Nukus.
- Fergana: the activity-code list was updated on 20 September. Returns stayed high after that. Intake staff say the usual missing item now is the workshop address, not the code.
- Samarkand: registration of a complete file is often finished the same morning. The second visit is the electronic-signature appointment, which is a different step.
- Tashkent City: portal applications are fast at the registration step. Several residents still wait days for the bank-account request that sits outside that step.
- All four directors objected to the draft sentence that every centre “finishes in 15 minutes”, because the centres do not use the same start and end time.

## What they asked the deputy to do

- Define the clock in the draft before anyone publishes it.
- Do not announce that SMS is nationwide.
- Leave the choice of a staff pilot versus a form pilot until the six-month table is on the table.
