# Tuesday task — brief the deputy before tomorrow’s meeting

Training exercise. Use only the files in this folder. Every group has the same pack. Your group’s priority is on the card the instructor gives you:

- Economy: put time-to-operate first
- Digital: put repeated returns and the form first
- Social: put access in Karakalpakstan first

You are the aide to the deputy of the Agency for Public Services. The meeting is tomorrow at 10:00. Title: why “15 minutes” is still not what residents experience.

Answer:

1. Where does the time actually go?
2. Which claims in `old-deck.md` are not supported by the other files?
3. What can be decided tomorrow, and what must wait?

Done:

- `briefing.md`, about two pages. After each conclusion, name the source file in parentheses.
- `slides.md`, exactly six slides: question, where the time goes, evidence, claims to delete, decision for tomorrow, items that must wait.

A person checks every number and every “already live” claim before you present.
