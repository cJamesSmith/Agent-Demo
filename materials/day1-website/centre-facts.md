# Public Services Centre No. 3, Samarkand

Training exercise. The centre, address, and phone number are fictional.

- Name: Public Services Centre No. 3, Samarkand City
- Address: 12 Mirzo Ulugbek Street, Samarkand
- Phone: +998 66 555 01 03
- Hours: Monday–Friday 09:00–17:00; Saturday 09:00–13:00; Sunday closed
- The intake desk stays open through lunch

## 1. Register as an individual entrepreneur

For a resident who will operate a business in his or her own name. A home sewing workshop can use this service. This is not registration of a company.

Bring:

- ID card or passport
- PINFL (personal identification number)
- mobile number
- address where the activity will take place
- activity type

A written lease is required only when the premises are rented. A lease is not required for a home the applicant lives in.

Two ways to apply:

- In person at Desk A of this centre
- Online after a OneID login, through business registration on the unified public-services portal (my.gov.uz)

Once the application is complete, the registration step is done in real time and takes no more than 30 minutes. That clock does not include obtaining a PINFL, obtaining an electronic signature, opening a bank account, or buying a cash register.

This centre does not charge a counselling fee.

## 2. PINFL, for a resident who does not have one

Bring the ID card or passport. The applicant comes in person. A relative cannot apply or collect the PINFL. When the identity document matches, this centre issues it the same day.

## 3. Electronic-signature appointment

Bring the ID card and the PINFL. The centre books the appointment. It does not hand over the signature on the spot. This pack does not state how many days the appointment takes.
