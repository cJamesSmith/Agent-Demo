# Routing rules — complaints unit

Training exercise. Fictional internal rules.

## Who owns what

| Owner | Takes | Deadline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Desk A, intake | Documents, what to bring, a file refused at intake, a registration that was not completed | 3 working days |
| Desk B, status | "Where is my file", a file open longer than the stated time, a missing notification | 3 working days |
| Portal team | The online form, activity codes, OneID login, an error message on my.gov.uz | 5 working days |
| Regional coordinator | Anything naming a service area outside Samarkand city, queue length, staffing, opening hours | 5 working days |
| Supervisor | Everything under "Escalation triggers" below | 2 working days to acknowledge |

A complaint that touches two owners goes to the one that can close it. If neither can close it alone, it is an escalation.

## Categories

- **Process** — the steps took too long, or the resident was sent back more than once.
- **Information** — the resident was told something wrong, or told nothing.
- **Access** — the resident could not reach the service at all: distance, hours, language, disability, no internet.
- **Conduct** — how a member of staff behaved.
- **Outcome** — the resident disputes a refusal or a decision.

## Escalation triggers

Send to the supervisor, the same day, when any of these apply:

1. A member of staff is accused of asking for a payment that is not an official fee.
2. The resident says they have complained about the same thing before and nothing happened.
3. The complaint alleges that a resident was refused on a ground not on the allowed list for that service.
4. The complaint concerns conduct toward a resident.
5. The file has been open more than 15 working days.
6. The resident has asked for a written decision they can appeal.

An escalation must include: the complaint, what the desk already checked, what it could not resolve and why, and the deadline that is running.

## Outside our agency

We do not decide complaints about a bank, a tax office, or a notary. We write to the resident naming the body that does decide, and we do not forward the complaint ourselves. The deadline for that reply is 3 working days.

## Notification

When a complaint is registered, the resident is sent an SMS with the case reference. The SMS service is live in every region as of 1 September 2026.

## What we never write

- "Incomplete documents" or "your request cannot be processed", with no specific reason.
- A resolution date we cannot meet.
- A statement that the matter is closed while any part of it is still open with another owner.
