# Desk notes — first week of November

Training exercise. Notes written by staff, not an official record.

## Desk B, status, Samarkand — note of 6 November

We cannot answer "where is my file" for anything that has gone to the regional coordinator. We see the file leave and we do not see it again until it comes back. Residents are told "it is with someone else" because that is all the screen says.

## Intake note, Samarkand — 4 November

The lease question keeps coming back. The rule is that a lease is required only when the room is rented, and not when the applicant lives at the address. Some officers are asking for it in every case. This has been raised twice at the morning meeting.

## Portal team note — 5 November

The activity-code list was updated on 20 September. Complaints about codes dropped after that. What we see now is the address line: the form accepts an empty workshop address and rejects the submission at the next step, with a message that does not say which field is wrong. A fix has been requested. It is not scheduled.

## Karakalpakstan coordinator note — 7 November

Status SMS is not reaching residents here. We have been raising it since September. Residents are told at the desk that they will receive an SMS, because that is what the intake script says, and then nothing arrives. Nine officers cover the whole region.

## Language note, Fergana — 5 November

We have no Karakalpak-speaking staff at this centre and the form has no Karakalpak version. When somebody needs it, we ask a relative in the queue to help. This is not a procedure, it is what we do.

## Saturday note, Samarkand — 7 November

The desk closes at 13:00 on Saturday. There is no rule about residents already in the queue at closing time. Different officers do different things. Last Saturday the queue was cut at 13:00 with four people waiting.

## Hotline note — 8 November

The hotline cannot see a resident's file. Operators can only take the complaint and pass it on. Residents believe the hotline can check a status, and we have never told them otherwise.

## Complaints unit note — 8 November

Six of this week's complaints repeat something we logged in September or October. We have no way to link a new complaint to an old one. Each arrives as if it were the first.
