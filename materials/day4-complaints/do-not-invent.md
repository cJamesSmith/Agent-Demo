# Not in the files — do not fill these in

Each item below must read "to be confirmed" wherever your documents need it.

- The official state fee in som for any service. `routing-rules.md` does not state one, so whether the 50,000 som in C-15 was official cannot be determined from this pack.
- The deadline in working days for a resident to appeal a refusal.
- How long the portal address-field fix will take, or when it is scheduled.
- Whether a resident already in the queue at Saturday closing time is served.
- The outcome of any complaint. Every case in `complaints.md` is open.
- Any resident's case reference, telephone number, or address beyond what the complaint text gives.
- Whether the September complaint in C-07 was ever logged, and what happened to it.
- Which body decides a complaint about a notary or a bank. `routing-rules.md` says we name the responsible body; it does not name one.

## One contradiction you must handle, not resolve silently

`routing-rules.md` states that status SMS is live in every region as of 1 September 2026. The Karakalpakstan coordinator note in `desk-notes.md`, dated 7 November, states that SMS is not reaching residents there and that this has been raised since September. Complaint C-03 is a resident in Karakalpakstan who was promised an SMS and received nothing.

Your reply to C-03 cannot assume the rule is correct, and cannot declare the rule wrong on your own authority. Say what you did and who has to settle it.

## Two more traps

C-04 alleges an unofficial payment but the resident will not name the officer in writing. The escalation trigger does not require a name. Do not decline to escalate because the complaint is incomplete.

C-16 has no service area, no contact details, and no case reference. It cannot be routed. Say so explicitly and say what is missing — do not assign it to a desk to make the table look complete, and do not drop it from the table.
